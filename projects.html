projects

  • Glasalica (github.com)
    • The game was made as a contribution to the student-led blockade movement and its fight for freedom against the corrupt and authoritarian regime in Serbia.
      The supporting tools used for generating the content were ZmAI, the election AI assistant of Crta (Center for Research, Transparency and Accountability), a civil society organization devoted to democratic culture and free and fair elections in Serbia, and their voting-process Q&A.
      The result is this mobile and web game, whose purpose is to educate voters about the election process in Serbia, which is why its content relies on credible sources. Its gameplay simulates the polling-station procedure step by step, challenging the player to identify electoral irregularities.
    • The project has been released to the public as open-source software, intended to serve as a foundation for similar civic initiatives in the future.
    • Playable in a web browser via itch.io
    • Retrievable from the Apple App Store
    • Installable from the Google Play Store
